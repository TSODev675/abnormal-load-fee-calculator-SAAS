🚛 Compliance Management

Stay up-to-date with South African transport regulations and ensure full compliance.

📋 Compliance Checklist

✅ Ensure all Abnormal Load Permits are approved by the Department of Transport.
📖 Follow TRH11 & NRTA Guidelines for oversized vehicle permits.
🛣️ Verify route approvals & bridge clearances before transport.
📏 Comply with SANRAL & municipal road weight limits.
🚛 Ensure Gross Vehicle Mass (GVM) is within legal limits.
⚖️ Use weighbridges to confirm compliance.
🚦 Follow speed limits for different vehicle classes.
🚓 Use escort vehicles where required for abnormal loads.
📜 Ensure all vehicles have valid roadworthy certificates.
🧾 Maintain SARS-compliant invoicing & customs clearance records.
📜 Ensure all compliance certificates are accessible.
📝 Keep detailed transport logs for inspections.
